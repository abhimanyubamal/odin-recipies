White Sauce Pasta

Description:

This creamy and delicious white sauce pasta is a perfect comfort food. Made with a

Ingredients:

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg (optional)
  • Chopped parsley for garnish

Steps:

  1. Cook the pasta according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the flour and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly to make a roux.
  3. Gradually whisk in the milk, ensuring there are no lumps. Continue to cook and stir until the sauce thickens, about 5-7 minutes.
  4. Remove the sauce from heat and stir in the Parmesan cheese, salt, pepper, and nutmeg (if using).
  5. Pour the sauce over the cooked pasta and toss to coat evenly.
  6. Garnish with chopped parsley and serve hot.
